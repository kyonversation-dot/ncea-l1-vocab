# NCEA Level 1 Japanese — Vocabulary Practice

**→ https://kyonversation-dot.github.io/ncea-l1-vocab/**

A self-serve practice page for NCEA Level 1 Japanese. Open it any time, on a phone or a laptop.
No account, no login, no data collected. Progress is kept in the browser.

## What is in it

| Deck | Cards | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Kanji words** | 94 | The words the official Kanji List actually names, joined to the Japanese→English sheet. Each card has the reading, the English, an example sentence and its translation. |
| **Topics** | 20 decks, 715 cards | All 585 listed words, grouped by the contexts the standards themselves name — school, family, hobbies, holidays (reading), food, daily routine, town, sport (listening) — plus verbs, describing words, question words and joining words. |
| **Numbers & dates** | 113 in 15 patterns | Times, dates, prices, counters. |

Every word comes from the **official NCEA Level 1 Japanese Vocabulary List (2026)** published by NZQA.
Nothing was invented or guessed. The example sentences are written by hand and then checked
back against the list by script, so no out-of-scope vocabulary appears.

## Why words, not characters

In the external assessments **every kanji carries furigana**, and the only kanji used are the
57 on the official list. That makes the reading paper, in practice, a vocabulary test — and the
listening paper contains no kanji at all. So the unit of practice here is the **word**
(`日曜日`, `一時間目`, `じゅぎょう中`), not the isolated character.

## Modes

- Kanji → reading
- Kanji → meaning
- Fill the sentence (the English translation is shown, so the blank is answerable)
- English → Japanese

Answers are shown the way the exam shows them: furigana above the word.

Keyboard: `space` reveals, `1` again, `2` got it.
