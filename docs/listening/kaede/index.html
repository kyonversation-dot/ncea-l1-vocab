The passage

Tama, a student from Aotearoa New Zealand, is talking to his Japanese friend Kaede about her day in Japan. The conversation is in four sections.

(a) What Kaede does, and what her family does

Kaede herself

  • whenGets up at 6:30
  • whyGets up early because school is forty minutes away by train
  • whereGoes to school by train
  • whatEats breakfast with her older brother
  • whatTakes a packed lunch
  • whenClasses start at 8:30
  • whenClasses finish at 3:30
  • whatEats her lunch with friends at lunchtime
  • whereReads books in the library
  • whenDoes homework at home Monday to Friday
  • whenClub is on Mondays and Thursdays
  • whenThere is a match this Saturday
  • whatPlays basketball at club
  • feelingClub is a lot of fun because she plays basketball
  • whenSunday is her only day off, and she sleeps until 10 in the morning
  • whatGoes shopping with her grandmother

Her family

  • whoHer GRANDMOTHER makes the rice and the eggs for breakfast
  • feelingSo it is delicious every day
  • whoHer older brother is a university student — he only eats breakfast with her
  • whyHer grandmother makes her packed lunch too, because Kaede is bad at cooking
  • feelingHer grandmother is very good at cooking

If you wrote that Kaede makes her own lunch, or that her brother makes breakfast, the subject slipped. Tracking WHO does WHAT is the whole point of this one.

  • Music is her favourite subject

  • Science is very difficult
  • Maths is EASIER than science — check you did not write it the other way round
  • whySo she likes maths
  • She does not like English very much

(c) Which day does Kaede like best, and why?

The answer is Sunday. She says so — but she never says why. You have to build the reason yourself out of all four sections. One connection is already good. Three or more and you are reaching Excellence.

  • On weekdays she is up at 6:30, because school is forty minutes away
  • On weekdays she does homework at home
  • There is a match on Saturday
  • Sunday is her ONLY day off — she can sleep until 10
  • On Sunday she goes shopping with her grandmother, so she spends it with someone she likes
