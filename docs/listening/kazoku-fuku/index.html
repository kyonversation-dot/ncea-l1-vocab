The passage

Shiori, a student in Japan, is talking to her friend Nikau in Aotearoa New Zealand about a birthday present for his sister. The conversation is in three sections.

Example answers

(a) Complete the table with information from the passage

Information about Nikau's mother

  • whatWorks at a clothes shop
  • whatAlways wears the shop's clothes, and is very stylish
  • whatHer shop is cheaper than other shops

Information about Nikau's sister

  • whenHer birthday is next Saturday; she wants new clothes
  • feelingLoves the clothes from her mother's shop
  • whatLikes blue and white clothes
  • whatHer high school has a uniform: a green jumper every day, and only black shoes
  • feelingDoesn't much like the uniform, so at the weekend she always wears clothes she likes
  • whoLast year Nikau gave her a scarf; she gave Nikau a big bag

The より fact goes one way only — it is the MOTHER's shop that is cheaper than other shops, not the other way round. And the giving goes in two different directions (あげました／くれました): Nikau GAVE his sister a scarf, and his sister GAVE Nikau a bag — if you wrote it the other way round, the 授受 direction slipped. Watch also for くろだけ (only black) — her shoes are not just black, they are ONLY black.

(b) What present will Nikau give his sister, and why?

  • A blue jumper, bought at his mother's shop, at the weekend
  • whyShe wants new clothes
  • whyShe likes blue
  • whyShiori suggested a blue jumper
  • whyJumpers are expensive, but his mother's shop is cheaper than other shops

(c) Is Nikau's sister likely to be happy with the present?

This is not stated in the passage — you have to judge from the evidence. Either "yes" or "not sure" can score well if it is supported by facts from the passage.

  • Evidence for "yes": she wants new clothes; she likes blue and white; she loves the clothes from her mother's shop, and the jumper comes from there; she doesn't much like the green school uniform, so a blue jumper is something she would wear at the weekend
  • Evidence a student might use for "not sure": last year she gave Nikau a big bag and he only gave her a scarf, so the present matters to her; a jumper is clothing she might compare with the uniform jumper she doesn't like
