GRAMMAR PRACTICE

あげる・もらう・くれる

Giving and receiving · 20 short examples and tasks

Follow the gift. Who gives it? Who receives it?

Me → someone: あげます
Someone → me / my family: くれます
Receiver ← giver: もらいます

For gifts between other people, use あげます when describing them from outside their group. くれます brings the gift towards the speaker or the speaker’s family.

私わたしは 先生せんせいから 本ほんを もらいました。
先生せんせいが 私わたしに 本ほんを くれました。

These describe the same gift. With もらいます, both から and に can mark the giver. With あげます / くれます, に marks the receiver. We are practising gifts of things here.
